## English (en)

### App name
Lidhikri

### Short description (80 chars)
Adhan, prayer times & after-prayer dhikr — auto tasbih, offline

### Full description
Lidhikri — your daily companion for dhikr and remembrance.

Do you forget to remember Allah while using your phone? Lidhikri plays the adhan at prayer times, reminds you with after-prayer adhkar, and plays short audio tasbih while you use your phone — without interrupting calls or chats.

✦ Prayer times
• Fard times for your city — search or current location
• Calculation method, Asr madhab, and daylight saving
• One city for times, adhan, and qibla

✦ Adhan
• Audio adhan at the start of each prayer you enable
• Muadhin voice, a file from your phone, or a short/silent alert
• Reminder before the adhan, and iqama after it
• After-adhan azkar (repeat the muadhin) when it ends
• Optional playback even in silent mode

✦ After-prayer adhkar
• One reminder from the after-prayer collection after each fard
• Minutes after the adhan — independent of the tasbih pause window
• Pause tasbih and audio adhkar from the adhan for a duration you choose per prayer

✦ Auto tasbih
• Periodic reminders (Subhan Allah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar, and more)
• Custom interval, random or sequential playback
• Pauses during phone and VoIP calls
• Optional pause during media, silent/Do Not Disturb, or by flipping the phone

✦ Auto adhkar
• Hisnul Muslim sections (morning, evening, sleep, and more)
• Daily schedule per section — time and weekdays you choose
• One dhikr from each enabled section, random or in order
• Seasonal takbir (Eid) via the Hijri calendar

✦ Flexible display
• Popup over other apps, silent text notification, or lock screen
• Audio only or audio + text
• Customizable popup size, position, and duration

✦ Digital misbaha
• Traditional beads or electronic counter
• Target count, sound, and vibration
• Home screen widgets (traditional 3×2 or electronic 2×2)

✦ Dhikr of the day
• A random daily dhikr from your collection
• Home widget or silent lock-screen reminder
• Colors, font size, and transparent background

✦ Read adhkar
• Browse Hisnul Muslim
• Reciter audio or TTS
• Select specific items to listen

✦ Voices
• Built-in audio, your own recording, or a file from your phone
• Separate reciter and volume for tasbih and for adhkar
• Download for offline use

✦ Custom dhikr stay on your device only — no account, no ads.

Works offline after downloading audio. Available in Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Turkish, Urdu, Indonesian, and Hindi. Light and dark themes. Comfortable layout on tablets.

———
After install: enable notifications, overlay permission, and battery exemption so reminders stay reliable.
