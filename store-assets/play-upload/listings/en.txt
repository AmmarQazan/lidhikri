## English (en)

### App name
Sabbih

### Short description (80 chars)
Auto dhikr, Hisnul Muslim, misbaha & home widgets — works fully offline

### Full description
Sabbih — your daily companion for dhikr and remembrance.

Do you forget to remember Allah while using your phone? Sabbih reminds you automatically with short audio tasbih and adhkar — without interrupting calls or chats.

✦ Auto tasbih
• Periodic reminders (Subhan Allah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar, and more)
• Custom interval, random or sequential playback
• Pauses during phone and VoIP calls
• Optional pause during media, silent/Do Not Disturb, or by flipping the phone

✦ Auto adhkar
• Hisnul Muslim sections (morning, evening, sleep, and more)
• Daily schedule per section — time and weekdays you choose
• One dhikr from each enabled section, random or in order
• Seasonal takbir (Eid) via the Hijri calendar

✦ Flexible display
• Popup over other apps, silent text notification, or lock screen
• Audio only or audio + text
• Customizable popup size, position, and duration

✦ Digital misbaha
• Traditional beads or electronic counter
• Target count, sound, and vibration
• Home screen widgets (traditional 3×2 or electronic 2×2)

✦ Dhikr of the day
• A random daily dhikr from your collection
• Home widget or silent lock-screen reminder
• Colors, font size, and transparent background

✦ Read adhkar
• Browse Hisnul Muslim
• Reciter audio or TTS
• Select specific items to listen

✦ Voices
• Built-in audio, your own recording, or a file from your phone
• Separate reciter and volume for tasbih and for adhkar
• Download for offline use

✦ Custom dhikr stay on your device only — no account, no ads.

Works offline after downloading audio. Available in Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Turkish, Urdu, Indonesian, and Hindi. Comfortable layout on tablets.

———
After install: enable notifications, overlay permission, and battery exemption so reminders stay reliable.
