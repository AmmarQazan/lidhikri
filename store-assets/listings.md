## English (en)

### App name
Sabbih

### Short description (80 chars)
Auto dhikr & adhkar with audio, digital misbaha, Hisnul Muslim — works offline

### Full description
Sabbih — your daily companion for dhikr and remembrance.

Do you forget to remember Allah while using your phone? Sabbih reminds you automatically with short audio adhkar and tasbih while you use your device — without interrupting calls or chats.

✦ Auto tasbih
• Periodic reminders (Subhan Allah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar, and more)
• Custom interval, random or sequential playback
• Pauses during phone and VoIP calls
• Optional pause during media playback

✦ Auto adhkar
• Hisnul Muslim sections (morning, evening, sleep, and more)
• Daily scheduled reminders per section
• Random or sequential dhikr from each section
• Seasonal takbir (Eid) via Hijri calendar

✦ Flexible display
• Popup over apps, silent text notification, lock screen
• Audio only or audio + text
• Customizable popup size, position, and duration

✦ Digital misbaha
• Count dhikr manually with a tap
• Set a target count

✦ Read adhkar
• Browse Hisnul Muslim
• Play with reciter audio or TTS
• Select specific items to listen

✦ Voices & reciters
• Built-in bundled audio
• Choose reciter, record your voice, or attach a file
• Download for offline use

✦ Your personal adhkar
• Add custom dhikr (4 languages)
• Stored locally on your device only

✦ Works offline after downloading audio.

After install: enable notifications, overlay permission, and battery exemption for reliable reminders.

### Keywords
tasbih, adhkar, dhikr, misbaha, hisnul muslim, islamic, reminder, morning adhkar
